Instructions

Annotation Protocol

This annotation protocol guides you to consistently classify how social media posts refer to scientific studies. You will determine the stance of a post towards the main conclusion of a scientific study.

Following this framework ensures your annotations are accurate and comparable with those from others. Spend up to 5 minutes to familiarize with the data, definitions, criteria, and examples. You should understand how to distinguish between the different stance categories before moving on to the training phase.

Data

You are given a reply post and the scientific paper (title + abstract) that was referenced in the initial Reddit submission post.

The reply post is always a direct reply to the submission post and the submission post does not contain any text apart from the title and the link of the paper.

⚪ No Stance

Annotate

if the reply post expresses no stance toward the main conclusion at all.


Note: only for posts that genuinely take no position on the conclusion, NOT for posts you find hard to read.
Common patterns (not exhaustive)
  • N1: Unclear connection to the paper: "Why doesn't the media report about it?"
  • N2: Posts directed at other users or the thread: e.g., "@userABC, why do you think that?" or "Thanks for posting"
  • N3: Mere mention or link provision: e.g., "This paper was already shared yesterday" or "Paywalled, preprint is here: [link]"
  • N4: Honest information-seeking questions: e.g., "What did they use for identifying the critical threshold?"

Step-by-step Decision

To arrive at consistent decisions, always follow these steps:

1. Does the post encourage readers to at least partially doubt, or dismiss the study's main conclusion? -> 🔴 Negative Stance

Notes:

  • posts may combine praise and criticism so that patterns from Negative Stance and Non-negative Stance are met. Negative Stance takes precedence if it is not rejected or outweighed within the post itself.
  • check that the Negative Stance is aimed at the study and not just at deniers, the sharer, corporations, politicians, or other commenters

2. If not, does the post express a stance toward the main conclusion that is not negative, i.e., it endorses, accepts, reports, or reacts positively to it? -> 🟢 Non-negative Stance

3. Otherwise, if the post expresses no stance toward the main conclusion at all -> ⚪ No Stance
