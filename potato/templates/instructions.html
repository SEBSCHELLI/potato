Annotation Protocol

This annotation framework guides you to consistently identify negative citations in social media posts. Following this framework ensures your annotations are accurate and comparable with those from others. Spend up to 5 minutes reading the definitions, criteria and examples. You should understand how to identify negative citations before moving on the training phase.

What are negative citations?

In this annotation project, we define negative citations in the following way:

A negative citation occurs when a social media post encourages readers to partially or fully question, doubt, or dismiss the conclusions of the referenced study.

Criteria for negative citations:

Post criticizes the study’s data, methodology, or interpretations, etc., and/or proposes better study design choices

Examples:

Post mentions flaws in the study’s design (e.g., sample too small, wrong method, claims too strong)
Post:
This study relies on only a 35-year velocity record and 6 years of GPS data at a single site ... hardly enough to make broad claims about the ice stream’s behavior.
