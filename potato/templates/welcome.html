Instructions

Welcome and thank you for participating!

In this task, you will help annotate social media posts that refer to scientific papers.

You will be shown Reddit reply posts that refer to a scientific paper, along with the title and abstract of the paper. Your task is to determine whether the post expresses criticism or questions the scientific study in a way that may lead readers to doubt all or part of the study’s findings and conclusions.

Example:

The figure on the right shows a Reddit submission from user UserABC referring to the paper “Very-low-carbohydrate diet enhances human T-cell immunity through immunometabolic reprogramming”. Another user, User123, replies (blue box) and implicitly criticizes the study by suggesting that a different study reports contradicting results.

During the annotation task, you will decide whether Reddit reply posts that refer to climate change related papers contains such criticism.

This session will take approximately 60 minutes and is structured as follows:

  1. Familiarization: Review the Annotation Protocol (10 minutes)
  2. Training: Annotate examples with feedback (15 minutes)
    During Training phase you need to annotate {{n_train_items}} posts. After each annotation, you will receive feedback. To pass the Training phase you are not allowed to make more than {{max_mistakes - 1}} mistakes. Otherwise, your participation ends, and you will be paid accordingly.
  3. Annotation: Annotate the full dataset (35 minutes)
    During Annotation phase you need to annotate {{n_items_to_annotate}} posts.
    Hidden among these are {{n_attention_checks}} attention checks (posts with known answers). You won't be able to tell which posts these are, so please annotate every post as carefully as you would any other. For each attention check post you annotate correctly, you earn a coin, worth {{coin_to_money}}.
  4. Finish: The return link to Prolific will be provided

Your annotations are valuable and help improve research.
